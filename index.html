About

I'm Philip — 22, based in Denmark. I edit and shoot short-form and campaign content, and I care about the whole chain: the hook, the pacing, the sound, the colour — not just cutting clips together.

Currently working as a media graphics apprentice at BET25 in Aarhus, alongside my own projects. I'm not just someone who's opened Premiere Pro a few times — I've got real output, a visual point of view, and a track record of making content that actually performs.

I also come at this from a design and dev background, not just an editing one — so I think about layout, type, and structure as much as I think about cuts. Give me a rough idea and I'll come back with something sharper than you pitched.

My journey

  1. Web Developer education

    Started out studying web development — learned how to build before I learned how to edit. That structured, technical thinking still shows in how I plan a cut.

  2. Graphic Designer education — finishing this November

    Currently wrapping up a Graphic Designer education. A good edit is also about typography, layout, and colour — not just the timeline.

  3. Started with Top Comfy — about a year ago

    My first real step into professional content work, outside of school.

  4. Launched my personal brand — 4–5 months ago

    Started building and publishing under my own name — sharing the process, not just the output.

  5. Now: media graphics apprentice at BET25

    Bringing design, development, and content together — day to day work on video and graphics for a real brand.
