About

Drop your photo in
assets/img/philip-placeholder.jpg

I'm Philip — 22, based in Denmark. I edit and design short-form and campaign content, and I care about the whole chain: hook, pacing, sound, colour, layout — not just cutting clips together.

I've been in the Adobe Suite for about four years — Premiere, After Effects, Photoshop, DaVinci Resolve. The last year or two has been professional: real briefs, real deadlines, real clients.

Currently a media graphics apprentice at BET25 in Aarhus, alongside my own projects. I've got real output, a visual point of view, and a track record of content that actually performs.

I come at this from a design and dev background too, not just editing — so I think about layout, type, and structure as much as I think about cuts. Give me a rough idea and I'll come back with something sharper than you pitched.

Outside of work I'm usually at the gym or out for a run — turns out I don't like sitting still, on a timeline or off it.

My journey

The short version of how I got here — a new milestone every few months.

  1. 01 Web Developer education

    Started out studying web development — learned how to build before I learned how to edit. That structured, technical thinking still shows in how I plan a cut.

  2. 02 Graphic Designer education — finishing this November

    Currently wrapping up a Graphic Designer education. A good edit is also about typography, layout, and colour — not just the timeline.

  3. 03 Started with Top Comfy — about a year ago

    My first real step into professional content work, outside of school.

  4. 04 Launched my personal brand — 4–5 months ago

    Started building and publishing under my own name — sharing the process, not just the output.

  5. 05 Now: media graphics apprentice at BET25

    Bringing design, development, and content together — day to day work on video and graphics for a real brand.
