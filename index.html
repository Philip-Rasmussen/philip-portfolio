About

Drop your photo in
assets/img/philip-portrait.jpg

I'm Philip — a 22-year-old video editor and graphic designer based in Aarhus. I like taking an idea from rough concept to final export.

At BET25, I work with graphic design, motion graphics and campaign production as part of an in-house marketing team.

Alongside that, I produce artist interviews and event content with Top Comfy and build short-form video projects under my own name.

I'm usually involved across the process — planning, filming, audio, editing, sound design and color grading. My background in web development also means I naturally think about structure, usability and how the final work is experienced — not only how it looks.

I spend a lot of time experimenting, learning new techniques and trying to make the next project better than the last. Away from the screen, I'm usually working out, running or doing something active.
