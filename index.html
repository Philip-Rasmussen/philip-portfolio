About

Drop your photo in
assets/img/philip-placeholder.jpg

I'm Philip — 22, based in Denmark. I edit and shoot short-form and campaign content, and I care about the whole chain: the hook, the pacing, the sound, the colour — not just cutting clips together.

I've been living in the Adobe Suite for about four years and know my way around all of it — Premiere, After Effects, Photoshop, Illustrator, Audition. The last year or two of that has been professional: real briefs, real deadlines, real clients.

Currently working as a media graphics apprentice at BET25 in Aarhus, alongside my own projects. I'm not just someone who's opened Premiere Pro a few times — I've got real output, a visual point of view, and a track record of making content that actually performs.

I also come at this from a design and dev background, not just an editing one — so I think about layout, type, and structure as much as I think about cuts. Give me a rough idea and I'll come back with something sharper than you pitched.
